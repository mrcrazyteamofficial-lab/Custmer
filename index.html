Frequently Asked Questions

Tap any question to see the answer.

How do I log in to my project?
Enter your unique Project ID on the home screen and tap Login. Enable “Remember me” to save your ID for faster access next time. If your project is still pending approval, please wait 24–48 hours.
How can I request an app update?
After login, open App Update Now from the Dashboard. Describe the changes you need, optionally attach a screenshot, and submit. You will receive a TRX ID to track your request. Status updates appear in Updates History.
How do I file a complaint?
From the Dashboard, tap Complaint. Select the issue type (User, Deposit, Withdraw, Plan, or Other), fill in the required details, and submit. Our team will review and reply; you can follow progress in Your Complaints history.
How do I check or pay my bill?
Open Check Bills on the Dashboard to view the current amount, date, and status. Tap Pay Bill, transfer via Meezan Bank or JazzCash, then enter your TRX ID. Status will move to Checking and then Paid after verification. You can also export a PDF summary.
How do I start a new project?
On the login screen, tap Create new project and complete the 4-step form (details, deadline, contact info, logo/design). After submission you will receive a unique Project ID. Keep it safe for future logins.
How can I contact support?
Use Contact Us on the login screen to open MrCrazy Customer Care (auto-reply bot), or tap Contact on WhatsApp for live assistance. WhatsApp: +977 976-1970136. You may also use live chat after phone login.
What happens when my request is completed?
When an update or complaint is marked Close/Complete, the next time you log in you will see a confirmation popup with the TRX ID. You can confirm and leave a star rating to help us improve our service.
